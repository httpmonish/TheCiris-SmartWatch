Engineering Blueprint

Bill of Materials & Hardware Specs

Every component verified against the physical schematic, pin maps, and firmware architecture.

01 / MCU Core
ESP32-S3 Microcontroller
Dual-core Xtensa® LX7 @ 240MHz, 512KB SRAM, 8MB PSRAM, Wi-Fi & BLE 5.0 mesh.
02 / Display
0.96" OLED SSD1306
128×64 monochrome high-contrast, non-blocking U8g2 hardware I2C driver (0x3C).
03 / Optical Sensing
MAX30101 Optical Sensor
Pulse oximeter & heart-rate monitor with Green & IR LEDs, ambient light cancellation (0x57).
04 / Motion IMU
BMI270 6-Axis IMU
Ultra-low power 16-bit 3-axis accelerometer + gyroscope with hardware step counter (0x68).
05 / Ambient Climate
SHT31 Temp & Humidity
Sensirion high-accuracy humidity ±2% RH and ambient temperature ±0.2°C (0x44).
06 / Clinical Body Temp
MAX30208 / TMP117
Dedicated ±0.1°C clinical epidermal skin temperature with direct thermal pad contact (0x50).
07 / Barometric & Flood
BME280 Pressure Module
Atmospheric pressure 300–1100 hPa, altitude tracking, and barometric drop flood alerts (0x76).
08 / Emergency Input
Tactile Panic Button
Waterproof silicone-sealed physical switch on GPIO 3 with hardware-debounced ISR interrupt.
09 / Audio-Haptic Alert
ERM Motor + Piezo Buzzer
ERM haptic feedback motor (GPIO 5) + high-decibel passive piezo acoustic siren (GPIO 6 LEDC).
10 / Solar Controller
CN3065 Charge IC
Complete CC/CV solar charging management IC with STAT pin telemetry output (GPIO 4).
11 / Photovoltaic
Flexible Solar Array
Compact multi-junction flexible solar panel seamlessly laminated along the wrist strap structure.
12 / Power Protection
Schottky Barrier Diode
Ultra-low forward drop (0.18V) reverse-current blocking — prevents battery drain into dark cells.
13 / Energy Storage
High-Density LiPo Cell
3.7V nominal lithium-polymer battery with precision voltage divider ADC sampling on GPIO 0.
14 / Resilience
Conformal Coating + Gasket
Silicone perimeter compression gasket with hydrophobic silicone PCB conformal barrier protection.
15 / Chassis
Matte-Black CNC Enclosure
Compact aerospace-grade polymer & aluminum bezel with integrated solar strap mounting channels.
16 / Interconnects
Gold-Plated PCB & FPC
High-reliability soldered flexible printed circuit jumpers and shielded I2C bus harness.
